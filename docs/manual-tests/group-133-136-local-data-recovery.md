# Groups 133-136 manual test - Local Data and Recovery

## Ordinary-mode health

1. Launch LifeOS Desktop in ordinary mode.
2. Open Settings, then Local Data & Recovery.
3. Confirm the mode label says Active data and no fictional records appear.
4. Confirm Agenda, Follow-ups, Work pipeline and Work sessions are listed.
5. Confirm missing stores report Missing without creating local files.

## Version and recovery safety

1. Save a record through each migrated feature and refresh the recovery view.
2. Confirm the store reports Healthy and schema 1.
3. Reset a migrated store and confirm its current file moves to Trash.
4. Confirm the entry shows its deletion and 30-day recovery deadline.
5. Restore only while the current store is missing; confirm overwrite is
   refused after a new current record exists.
6. Confirm unreadable, wrong-store and newer-schema files are preserved or
   rejected without silent replacement.

## Boundaries

- Do not claim unlisted modules use the new spine.
- Do not expose personal paths or record content in evidence.
- Do not permanently delete Trash entries during this checkpoint.
